# 🛡️ CyberGraph AI

### An Intelligent Cybersecurity Investigation Agent Powered by Knowledge Graphs

**CyberGraph AI** is an AI-powered cybersecurity investigation platform that connects security alerts, devices, vulnerabilities, users, and critical assets into a knowledge graph to help security teams investigate incidents and understand potential attack paths.

Instead of analyzing security alerts in isolation, CyberGraph AI explores the relationships between entities to identify suspicious connections, assess potential risks, and generate human-readable investigation reports.

Built for **Graph Hacks**, CyberGraph AI demonstrates how graph databases and intelligent agents can make cybersecurity investigations more connected, explainable, and efficient.

---

## 🚨 Problem Statement

Modern cybersecurity environments generate large volumes of alerts from different devices, applications, and security systems.

Security analysts often need to investigate these alerts individually and manually connect information about affected devices, vulnerabilities, users, and business assets.

This creates several challenges:

* **Alert overload:** Security teams receive numerous alerts and may struggle to prioritize critical incidents.
* **Disconnected information:** Relationships between alerts, devices, users, and vulnerabilities can be difficult to identify.
* **Complex investigations:** Understanding how an attacker could move between connected systems requires time and effort.
* **Limited explainability:** An alert alone may not reveal why an incident is dangerous or which assets are at risk.

## 💡 Our Solution

CyberGraph AI transforms disconnected cybersecurity information into a connected knowledge graph.

The platform links related security entities and helps investigators understand the context surrounding an alert. It can present investigation findings as a structured, human-readable narrative that explains the incident, its related entities, potential risks, and recommended next steps.

The goal is to move cybersecurity investigations from isolated alerts toward **relationship-driven, explainable threat analysis**.

---

## ✨ Key Features

### 1. 🔍 Security Alert Investigation

Analyze security alerts using their severity, affected assets, and relationships with other entities.

### 2. 🕸️ Knowledge Graph Investigation

Represent cybersecurity entities and their relationships in a graph structure powered by FalkorDB.

The graph can connect:

* Security alerts
* Devices and servers
* Vulnerabilities
* Users and accounts
* Critical business assets

### 3. 🔗 Relationship-Based Threat Analysis

Explore connections between alerts, vulnerable systems, user accounts, and important resources to identify potentially significant investigation paths.

### 4. ⚠️ Risk Context and Prioritization

Use available alert severity and relationship information to help investigators prioritize incidents that may affect critical systems.

### 5. 🧠 AI-Assisted Investigation

Support the investigation workflow by organizing connected evidence and turning technical findings into understandable explanations.

### 6. 📝 Story-Based Investigation Reports

Present findings in a human-readable investigation narrative covering the alert, related entities, potential attack paths, risks, and suggested defensive actions.

### 7. 🖥️ Interactive Web Interface

Provide a centralized interface for exploring cybersecurity alerts and reviewing investigation results.
